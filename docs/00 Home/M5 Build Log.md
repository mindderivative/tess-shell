## Differences from the Specs

All concern [D-Bus Service Layer](../40%20Specifications/Integration/D-Bus%20Service%20Layer.md) and [Module Context](../42%20Python%20API/Module%20Context.md) (full list in `/tmp/frame-review/M5A.md`):

- A call to a service with no owner raises `ServiceUnavailable` (with `.state`) instead of returning `UNAVAILABLE`; the state is also on `bus.state(name)`.
- `proxy()` starts the watch; an unread service counts as unavailable, so `available` starts false.
- A service absent at start gets no `on_service_lost`.
- `call` takes the D-Bus signature of its arguments (no introspection).
- `ctx.thread` does not exist yet; `Bus.run` and `Bus.run_blocking` serve workers.
- The service thread now always starts (before: only with `Follow_Desktop`); it connects to nothing until used.
- The "session bus unreachable at start: exit with an error" rule of the spec is not built; the portal reader logs one warning, uses defaults and retries in the background.
- The shell owns no bus name yet (IPC not built), so nothing re-requests one; `Bus.on_reconnect` is where the owner registers.
